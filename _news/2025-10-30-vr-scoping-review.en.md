Our latest scoping review, _"Toolboxes for Creating Immersive Virtual Reality Experiments in Psychological, Behavioral, and Cognitive Research,"_ is now available as a preprint on the SSRN archive! This work systematically evaluates the current landscape of VR experimental frameworks, offering a comprehensive guide for researchers looking to integrate immersive tech into their cognitive and behavioral studies.
